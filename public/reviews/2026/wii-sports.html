Synopsis

Wii Sports is the launch title for the Nintendo Wii and came bundled in with every purchase of a non-Japanese Wii. The game is currently the fourth best-selling game of all time, staggering at a massive 83 million amount of units sold worldwide. The game is widely acclaimed for its ease of access to people unfamiliar with gaming and its innovating motion controls. The game was originally never meant to be bundled in with the Wii for free, it was the idea of former Nintendo of America president Reggie Fils-Aime. He reportedly fought tooth-and-nail for the inclusion to happen, as he believed the Wii wouldn't sell well if there was nothing to play on it (in which he was right). The game is considered to be one of the most well-known and liked games of all time. The game is the first game to utilise Miis, the avatar system made for the Wii and all subsquent Nintendo consoles.


Art

Wii Sports takes on a very liminal art style. It's simple, but it works really well! It shares it's art style with the Mii Channel. The style uses simple shapes and visually appealing viusals with sparkly UI elements and charming Mii models. The game oozes with fun and whimsy and the animations move joyously! The game has this weird nostalgic factor I can't find in any other game, even when you ever grew up playing it. This game definitely hammered Miis into Nintendo's public image for a while, which says a lot on its own.


Gameplay

Miis

Wii Sports has Miis. And not just some Miis. But a HELL of a lot of Miis! They are everywhere in this game! The Miis serve as an amazing atmosphere for the game. It's so cool to be playing a game and then seeing your relatives or freinds walking around in the background. Also, they're just really heckin' cute.


Minigames

The main game has a total of 5 minigames. Here is a mini review for each of them:

Tennis
This is one isn't really my cup of tea. I find it kind of hard to get the game to recognise that I swinged, which causes me to swing super hard, which then results me to get an out. I don't really get it, but it's still a lot of fun with freinds.
Baseball
Okay this one is better than Tennis but not perfect. It gets super, super repetitive after a while. It's literally just "bat the ball when it's near" and "throw the ball when your opponent isn't looking". Still, really enjoyable, just gets stale very fast.
Bowling
Okay, this is when it gets good! Wii Sports Bowling just never gets old. It's so dumbfoundingly simple yet so effective, especially with other people! It's an absolute blast, and probably my favourite minigame in the game.
Golf
This one is also pretty good. It's very fun and is probably the best one to play with friends. It gets really funny at times. Only complaint is that is gets a bit sloggish in the earlier stages.
Boxing
This one is fun. It lets you beat up your siblings! Now that's my idea of fun!


Training and Fitness Test

Training is really fun. It's basically just a singleplayer mission mode where you can take on different specific objectives in each minigame. These are really fun to tackle by yourself and are unironically very good ways to improve your skills in the game. The Fitness Test is a mode that chooses three random missions from Training and make you do them back-to-back. After you're done playing, it'll give you an "age" based off how good you were. It refreshes every day and only one user can play per day. This is really fun, since it's random every time, although the statistics aren't really something I care about. (I'm totally not salty that the game said I was 53...)


Music

You know Wii Sports' soundtrack already. It's that iconic. But I'll describe it anyway. It's amazing! The music feels streamlined and proper, but still fun and exciting. Even after all these years it still feels novel! It's by far one of the most unique things about the game.

Verdict

Wii Sports is an instant classic is one of the most iconic games of all time. But, it is pretty lackluster, it being a tech demo after all. For that reason, I'm giving this game an 8/10.
