Synopsis

The Elephant Collection is a compilation of a series of remastered flash games made by jmtb02 themed around the titular blue elephant. These many flash games were originally uploaded to Newgrounds and the official Armor Games website for free, which they still are! The Elephant Collection simply just brings all of those games into a concise, neat, little offline executable.


Art

The art direction in The Elephant Collection varies greatly between games, as the compilation features titles released years apart. The main newly added content for the compilation specifically sports a very cute, soft, and pastel art style. The art in the actual games is very primitive, janky, and very simple, which is fine, these games are literally older than me. I'm not going to bully a game intended to be played on bloody Internet Explorer! I think the ugly art is actually kind of charming! It reminds me of when I used to play old ass browser games on my mom's crappy laptop.


Gameplay

Memories

Memories are little unlockable blurbs of text describing the development trivia of whatever game you unlocked it from. These memories are unlocked by completing goals within the game. This is a really nice way of encouraging the player to dig deeper when playing the games, and also give a great understanding of how the development of these silly little games played out!


Achievement Unlocked

Achievement Unlocked is my personal favourite of the games in the collection. In the Achievement Unlocked games, you play as the elephant jumping around various rooms collecting achievements. The twist? Almost everything gives you an achievement. For example, moving left is an achievement. And moving right is one, too. I think you're starting to see the appeal now. So now you have to start messing around interacting with everything in a very fun but also chaotic way. It's an incredibly fun and interesting mechanic that I haven't really seen any other game do as thoroughly. Out of the three games, I would say that Achievement Unlocked 2 is my favourite. The different layers of rooms were really fun to explore.


This Is The Only Level

This Is The Only Level is a game with only one level. The twist, however, is that the game makes you replay that same level with a new gimmick every time. It's a really fun and novel concept, but in exection it's probably the most lacklusting game of the decalogy. A lot of the levels have been worn off by age, as in they manipulated the advantage of being a browser game. Which this game isn't. So a bunch of the levels had to be redesigned and the solutions are quite frustrating to figure out. The rest of the game is still great to play, though.


Other titles

Obey! the Game is surpisingly addictive, and I really love the visuals in Run Elephant Run, it's super charming and really reminds me of that specific era of flash games. Elephant Quest is an amazing time killer, especially with the New Game+! Ending spoilers (Yes, this game has an actual ending...): That final rave at the end was awesome. It had me smiling like an idiot when all of the different elephants came into the screen.
