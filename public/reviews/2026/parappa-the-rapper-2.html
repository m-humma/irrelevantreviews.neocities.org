Synopsis

Parappa the Rapper 2 is the third installment (yes, THIRD installment) of the Parappa the Rapper series. Unlike the previous two games which were originally publshed for the PS1, Parappa the Rapper 2 was the first and only installment to be released for the PS2. The game features a total of 8 stages, a new high for the series, and new features like difficulty levels and multiplayer modes. The game is also much, much better looking and plays way more responsively. Just like the prior two games, the game was directed by Masaya Matsuura and art direction done by Rodney A. Greenblat.

"They [Sony] really wanted Parappa. Two years later Sony got their wish -- Parappa 2, for PlayStation 2. It was nice to return to Parappa. I was glad to be designing with him [Matsuura] again. Unfortunately I felt the script to Parappa 2 was too complicated. What I wanted to do with the game was different from Matsuura [had in mind]."

—Rodney A. Greenblat, on PaRappin’ With Rodney Greenblat

Art

The art direction in Parappa the Rapper 2 is built off of that featured in the prior games. The art style fundamentally stays the same, but the shading and models have been reworked to be in higher quality. The art style looks amazing, it feels new while still feeling like the same 'ol Parappa. The animations feel smoother than before, the graphics just pop! Not only that, but the UI is much, muuuuuch better now. The progress bar in the past two games was fine, but the one in Parappa the Rapper 2 is simply just leagues better. Symbols that you've pressed will be stamped onto the bar, making freestyling much easier since you have a good graph of your previous inputs.


Gameplay

Story

The story in every Parappa game is usually pretty funny and interesting to watch, so I'm happy to say that in my opinion Parappa the Rapper 2 has one of the funniest in series. The stories were always weird, but this game cranks it up to eleven! It's so ridiculous that you can't help but find it funny!


Revamped gameplay

The actual gameplay in Parappa the Rapper 2 compared to the original Parappa the Rapper and subsequential Um Jammer Lammy is like night and day. The game has transferred from a janky, broken and at times frustrating mess to an actual competent scoring system! When you play the game, you have to press the buttons on your PlayStation controller in time to a map put on the top of the screen. In the original games, the scoring system felt stupendously unfair to the point of near irritation. On the other hand, this game actually scores you consistently and doesn't shun you out so easily. The game feels fun. Yes, I know the bar isn't very high, but my point still stands, okay!? TL;DR, they made the game bearable to play. Yay.


Additional features

Parappa the Rapper 2 adds many new features that prior entries lacked. When you beat the final level, Parappa starts to wear a blue beanie. Now when you play a level, the level's chart is significantly harder. This repeats and Parappa's beanie turns pink, then finally yellow. This is a really, really nice way of implementing replayability. Not just that, it also adds a level of variety to every stage. Another feature they added was Multiplayer co-op and a versus computer mode. In this mode, you can play against a freind or CPU where you and your opponent take turns freestyling a song. This is really fun, especially against the CPUs. The later difficulty stages for the computer can get really challenging and fun.


Music

Obviously a rhythm game would have good music, but MAN does this game have great music! The music is upbeat and catchy, and sounds even better with the better sound quality of the PS2. The music is what makes the game feel alive! (I unironically have some of these in my playlist...)

Verdict

Parappa the Rapper 2 is an amazing sequel that fixes basically all of the problems I had with the prior game. For that reason, it gets a solid 8.5/10.
