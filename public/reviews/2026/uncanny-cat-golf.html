Synopsis

Uncanny Cat Golf is a very serious game. Uh huh, absolutely super serious. *wink wink* Okay, just kidding, this is the stupidest game I've ever played and I love it. The game revolves around a lot of internet culture, particularly a mesh of old 90's dial-up internet graphics with references to 2000's media and modern internet humour. The game is basically just a love letter to the internet as a whole. Okay, I know this still must look really stupid and unserious, but the game is really good, I swear!


Art

The art direction in Uncanny Cat Golf is incredibly wacky. You could probably already tell that. As mentioned before, the game relies hevaily on internet culture when referring to its identity. The game's assets are mostly combined of whatever the stupidest JPEGs the developer could find on the internet. In all honesty its an ugly, overstimulating and frankly juvenile look. But that's what makes it fun. It's nice to play a game that doesn't take itself so seriously once in a while. Also, it's just really funny.


Gameplay

Main Campaign

In the game, you control the semi-titular cat Canny, as you putt him around multiple wacky and zany golf courses. The twist, though, is that there multiple UNcanny cats (Uncannies?) that relentlessly chase you around the map. If you touch one of these uncannies, you get jumpscared and restart the level. Your Canny cat can bounce off of walls, interact with switches and projectiles, and can vary its speed depending on how far you let your mouse go. For a such a practical joke of a game, the game handles its controls and physics enginuity surprisingly well. The progression in difficulty feels very natural, and the game very rarely ever feels unfair or frustrating. Not only that, it's surprisingly addicting. I've went off-track and got myself stuck playing the game multiple times while writing this review...

Another one of the suprisingly well done features is Ranks. After beating each level, you're given a unique rank depending on your time and par. Each of these 5 ranks give you canny prisms, in which the better rank the more prisms. These prisms as well as a number of other objectives unlock items in the Summer Villa, which I will get to. This is not only a wondeful way of implementing replayablity, but also a great way of encouraging players to go for a certain high score on levels.


Summer Villa

The Summer Villa is where all the afforementioned unlockables such as costumes and minigames are kept. The minigames are surprisingly fun, which some of them unironically keeping me occupied. The costumes are great too, and there's even a Cat Artist tool which lets you make your cat! It seems like more of a side thing, but I honestly think that my playtime was split half-and-falf between the main campaign and Summer Villa.


Endless and Custom Levels

The endless mode is a mode that gives a random order of levels found in the campaign, as well as a few extras, and lets you play them for as ever long you survive. There is a timer, and the timer increases every time you beat a level. You can also skip levels, but these are limited and also penalize you. Right off the bat this already sounds super fun. There's an extra feature, a thermometer. Every time you die or skip a level, the thermometer goes up. And if the thermometer is filled, an angry, dangerous and more powerful red uncanny chases you alongside the other regular ones. This quote on quote "Red Demon" doesn't kill you, but it takes off time every time you hit it. This mode is just amazing. It's by far my favourite thing about the game. Super fun, and super addicting. Custom Levels are, well, custom levels. It's a cool feature but I haven't toyed too much with it to be able say anything about it.


Music

The music in Uncanny Cat Golf is way too good for its own good. Genuinely, why is there such good music in such a stupid looking game? I don't get it! Just listen to it for yourself and you'll get what I mean!

Verdict

Uncanny Cat Golf is an unexpectedly, mind-bogglingly, stupendously, an uncannily great game. Seriously, this thing is FREE! You can download it RIGHT NOW! Unironically one of the most unexpected finds I've ever made. No notes, instant 10/10. Remember to stay Canny!
