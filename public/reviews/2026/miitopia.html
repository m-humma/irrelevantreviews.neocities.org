Synopsis

Miitopia is a game with Miis. I think that tells you enough on how why this game is so good, but I'll still elaborate anyway. Miitopia is an incredibly fun and charming game. The dialogue is funny and witty, the art is inclining, the music is oozing with life, and the game is overall just weird in the best way possible. It always has you on the edge of your seat and defines why Miis are so beloved in the first place. It's so mind-numbingly simple yet has more content than most other games of its time. Miitopia is a simple little traditional RPG with the main gimmck being that almost every character can be made into anyone you'd like, like family or real-life freinds, using the functionality of Miis to its fullest. The antics your Miis get up to within your adventure is, to put it lightly, amusing. Okay, no, I mean really freaking silly. Like... reallyyyy freaking silly. And funny. And charming. And soulful. And... you get the gist.


Gameplay

Story and Premise

The story in Miitopia is weeiiirrdd. All of the residents of Miitopia have lost their faces, and have had them stuck to the heads of evil monsters by the Dark Lord, the literal definition of malice. It's up to you, yes, YOU, to rescue everyone's faces and give them back to their rightful owners with the help of your teammates and a few good-luck charms. And God. Don't ask.

As said before, YOU get to choose who the hero, your teammates, and just about anyone in the game to be whoever you want. This is done by making almost every role in the game super generic -- e.g., The Great Sage, or the Prince from a Faraway Land. It might sound bad, but it in turn lets you do basically whatever you want. Make the princess your dad, make the Dark Lord your baby cousin, make the great sage Mario! Everything still works in tandem, yet simultaneously chaotic at the same time. I love it.

Unfortunately though, the only thing preventing Miitopia from being a 10/10 is the underlying repetitiveness of it. Spoilers: In the game you will lose your teammates and job multiple times from the Dark Lord. Because of this, you will basically play the same exact game again multiple times, only difference being a different setting and story. This is really, really annoying, and unfortunately the reason a lot of people drop the game early.


RPG elements

The main gameplay loop is quite fun. Find a town, play levels, rescue the faces, and defeat the bosses. The battle system is quite generic, but still fun. You have a simple HP and MP system, that uses both regular and special magic attacks. There are several Jobs, Miitopia's take on roles in traditional RPG games, which give you special outfits and attacks. There are also special mechanics unique to Miitopia, like sprinkles that add certain affects or points to Miis, and Quirks which are little characteristics that randomly occur that depend on your Mii's personality type. Everything is in good fun and never feels too grindy, although that is still an option if that's your kind of thing.


The Miis and Dialogue

Miis are built into the Nintendo 3DS and Nintendo Switch systems, but Miitopia lets you customise them further. You can add things like personality types, war cries, favourite foods, and even more. The Miis you make are also incredibly talkative. There are charming encounters they can have with each other either out adventuring or in a hotel, which manipulates things like relationship statuses that add more benefits and content to the playthrough. You can send them out on outings, or watch them quip a witty line while watching them go adventure outsite. Miitopia without Miis is like a burger without a patty. Sad, pathetic, and bland. Which is why I love the Miis in this game so much! The art style is cute, as it's expanded upon the pre-existing art style of the Miis. It's cute, but it doesn't stand out too much.


Post-Game content

After you finish the game, there's somehow still so much to explore! There are two additional worlds with two more unlockable jobs, and many smaller bite-sized quests to do in the Hub you unlock late into the game. There are medals that act like achievements, and records that you can fill out and complete. There is also a gauntlet-like stage in the game named the "Tower of Dread" -- a fun boss rush of many smaller districts that you need to play before unlocking the actual tower. There is so much content packed into this game it's insane. Miitopia was ported to Switch in 2021 and came in with a whole lot of post-game content that I absolulutely love. This includes:

  • The Tower of Despair is an even harder tough-as-nails boss rush unlocked after the Tower of Dread. It's probably my favourite thing in the game by far.
  • Quality of life changes make the experience a whole lot better here on the Switch version.
  • Makeup. Yassify your Miis. Need me to say more?
  • Horse. That's it they just added a horse. Neighhhh.


Music

Miitopia has incredibly wacky music. Like, really wacky music. It's fun, weird, and encapsulates the whole game pretty well in my opinion. I also appreciate the music room that lets you listen to the whole OST in-game, I'll definitely listen to the OST through my switch and not just listen to illegally dumped song files published online. Totally.

Verdict

Miitopia is a quirky, charming game with a hell of a lot of silliness. It's fun, funny, and unique, which is why this game gets an outstanding 9/10.
