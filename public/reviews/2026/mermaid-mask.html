Synopsis

The Mermaid Mask is the third canon game in the Detective Grmioire series, following in the footsteps of its predeccessor: Tangle Tower. The story takes place after the events of the prior game, where Detective Grimoire and his companion Sally are hired to investigate the murder of Magnus Mortuga -- the former captain of the titular Mortuga Submarine. The game is more or less simply another Tangle Tower, sharing almost the exact gameplay mechanics and art style. This isn't a bad thing by any means, though! Even though I do wish the game tried to be unique when compared to its predecessor, I personally think the grander story more than makes up for it. The game is noticeably higher quality, too.


Art

Artwork in the Mermaid Mask is even better than before. The linework is smooth, the characters are wonderfully animated. Not only that, but a new medium has been introduced: 3D modelling. The 3D models are used for clues and recreaction scenes. The 3D models look amazing, as with everything in this game. They did a wonderful job at converting the pre-existing art style into 3 dimensions, especially as they didn't have much to work off of.

"I also learned some new skills during Crow Country -- 3D modelling, for instance, which I’ve put to use in various small ways within The Mermaid Mask. To be clear, I should say I didn’t model the beautiful 3D clues; they were modelled by our 3D artist, Kindra Dantone, and the 3D objects that feature in a few of the puzzles were modelled by our puzzle artist Kitt Byrne."

—Adam Vian, on The Mermaid Mask Creative Director on Making a Sequel

Gameplay

Story

In my opinion, the Mermaid Mask has a much better story than Tangle Tower, which is impressive considering that I absolutely loved the story for Tangle Tower! The story is extremely interesting, and almost always keeps you on your toes. I've found myself playing for way longer than I should be just because the story is THAT interesting. Every single clue, every single slightly important piece of dialogue tips you over into an entrance and before you know it, it's 2 a.m. and you aren't even feeling tired!


Characters

I absolutely adore the characters in the Mermaid Mask. The range of characters makes the game feel incredibly unique and fun. It can stretch from a pessimistic writer who struggles to find a plot for his next book, a poetic yet oddly solemn illusionist who's shrouded in mystery when regarding her background, an awkward yet weirdly socialable navigator who's running a dead-ended shop -- I could go on! The characters are weird and at times confusingly set up, which makes the entire investigator gameplay style so fun!


Quality-of-life changes

Compared to its predeccessor, the Mermaid Mask doesn't really bring many new things to the table, which is probably my only major complaint with the game. Although the game remains mainly the same, there are some new details and quality-of-life changes. My favourite change is the implementation of 3D models with clues and recreation scenes. The models used for clues are a fun addition and help make the game feel more immersive. Not only that, it also forces the player to be a lot more thorough when looking at clues. There's also new descriptions that let you view the text on certain clues -- which is insanely helpful considering that the text imprinted on clues have a much grander significance than compared to Tangle Tower. There's also been a few quality-of-life changes to the UI that make the game overall more enjoyable to play.


Puzzles

Compared to Tangle Tower, I personally think that the Mermaid Mask has the weaker puzzles of the two. Not to say that the puzzles in the Mermaid Mask are bad, but I defintely prefer the ones played in Tangle Tower. Most of the puzzles are on par with the ones in previous entries, fun and unique as always, but there's a few rotten eggs that kind of make you feel cheated on.


Music

The music in the Mermaid Mask is just as good as it was in Tangle Tower. It really sets the mood and describes each character that its trying to represent almost seamlessly! There isn't really much to say, since I have almost the exact thoughts I had with Tangle Tower's music.

Verdict

The Mermaid Mask is a wonderful sequel and murder-mystery game that truly does everything it sets out to do wonderfully. It oozes with life and is beautiful to both play and see. For that reason, I give this game a 10/10.
