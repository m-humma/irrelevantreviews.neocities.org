The History of My Site

Well, hello. This my first quote on quote "proper" blog post. This is a bit awkward, but here goes! I've decided to make the first blog post a short little document on how this site even came to be. It, uh, kinda makes sense. Doesn't it? (Please say it does...)


This isn't actually my first website on neocities. I used to have an old one. I made it to practice my HTML and CSS skills. Will I share this site? No. Never. But just know it's on the Wayback Machine. Well, after a few months of maintenance of that site, I got bored. I couldn't think of anything to add to my site. So, naturally, it sat abandoned for half a year. And so my interests drifted. At the time, I had found a neat little site named the Reading Project. This site is a book reviews site, where the reviews are made by a passionate group of people and volunteers. I found it so cool that so much content was available on a Neocities site! So, as you could very easily predict, this very much inspired me to make games reviews site. So I got to work.


My first review was Rhythm Tengoku. I chose that game since I was really into the Rhythm Heaven series at the time (and still now). Then, when I was done making the site, I saved a copy of my old site and then deleted it (it's a neocities policy) and uploaded the new, shiny website to Neocities!


Well, that website evolved, and has turned into the one that you're on right now! I've made a lot of awesome interactions here on Neocities and I've met a countless amount of nice people. I just absolutely adore all the cool sites that people online run, and I hope you enjoy one this much as I do! Well, that's it. I'll see you the next time I decide to make a blog post!


—Humma
