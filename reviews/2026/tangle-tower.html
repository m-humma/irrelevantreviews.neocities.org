Synopsis

Tangle Tower is a murder-mystery puzzle game developed and published by SFB Games. It is a follow-up to prior entry simply named Detective Grimoire, an old web game (when those were still around). The game follows the murder of Freya Fellow, the resident of the titular Tangle Tower -- a joint residency between two families: the Fellows and the Pointers. You play as an amateur detective named Grimoire, alongside his assistant Sally. As you go along the game, you learn more about the people who live here, alongside their lifestyles and relationships with other tenants. You discover rivalries, freindships, and a TON of family drama as you go around questioning family members about clues and their involvement with the murder. The game also includes puzzles in certian parts of the game. Tangle Tower is what I'd call a perfect murder-mystery game. Funny dialogue, intriguing plot points, gorgeous art and head-scratching puzzles. There isn't much else to improve from there.


Art

Tangle Tower has absolutely gorgeous artwork. The art style is pure eye-candy to look at. The animation is smooth and full of emotion, and colours clash in all the best ways. The portraits drawn for clues and items in the game are painted almost impeccably, and the rooms each character resides in are all drawn and rigged to perfection. I cannot overstate how phenomenal the artwork in this game is.

"The look of Tangle Tower naturally evolved as lead artist Catherine Unger and I worked together. We both felt like we'd levelled up our skills since last time and wanted to deliver something fresh, unique and memorable. The art style has to allow for a mixture of silly humour and sincere, serious moments, so it can be quite a balancing act. [...] In terms of inspirations for the art style... from my end, I think I'd mostly cite Tove Jannson and Genndy Tartakovsky."

—Adam Vian, on Behind the scenes: The making of Tangle Tower

Gameplay

Story

The game's storyline is extremely compelling and intriguing. There are many twists and turns along the story that mess up your perception of the case, which forces you to be much more mindful and doubtful even when it comes to things seen as "concrete evidence". As said before, when covering the case, you also stumble along deep family issues and a lot of drama. The more you play, the more understanding you gain on how the Fellows and Pointers came to be but also Tangle Tower as a whole. The second half gets really, and I mean really good. It feels super intriguing as you go along discovering more and more about the environment around you, which even includes the more inhumane acts. This game is super good at hooking you on, and once you play, you never stop until you've uncovered every nook and cranny of the game.


Characters

The residents of Tangle Tower are very entertaining to learn about. All of them manage to be unique in their own ways yet also nicely blend in with each other. As you play the game more and interrogate the characters further, you learn more about their personal lives as well as habits or personal ideologies. They also have quite intriguing relationships with each other, some sweeter than others. When I first started playing, I thought that attempting to learn the entire family tree would be a nightmare, but as you play more the relationships start to form into your head and by the end of the first few chapters you have a good idea on who's relationships are with who.


Clues and Interrogation

In the game, you can travel around each room in the house. Once you're in a room, you can click any part of the screen and Grimoire and Sally will make a small comment on whatever you clicked. The dialogues are usually very charming and funny. If the thing you clicked is important enough, it will be either marked as a clue or will let you into a puzzle, which for the latter I'll get to in a second. Clues are logged into your case file and have unique descriptions describing them. These clues can then be used to interrogate the residents of Tangle Tower. If something they say susses themselves or another person, then you can suspect them and do a more proper invesitgation. This is a super fun and unique mechanic that I haven't seen anywhere else. It's fun and conclusive, while still leaving some effort for the player in the act of finding those clues.


Puzzles

I have mixed feelings about puzzle games... which is a great thing considering that Tangle Tower is on the good side! Tangle Tower is amazing at doing puzzles. If you didn't know, the studio that made this game also made the Nintendo Switch game Snipperclips, a personal favourite puzzle game of mine. The puzzles in Tangle Tower are very fun to solve. They usually act as an item that needs to be unlocked or decoded to progress in the story. Usually these puzzles are extremely well thought-out and never feel as if their solution was cheap or not obvious enough, which is an issue I have with many other puzzle games. They're defenitely my favourite part of the game.


Music

Tangle Tower has an excellent soundtrack. The tracks usually perfectly stes the mood of whatever it wants it to be. It can be ambient, whimsical, gloomy, or even at times slightly magical whenever the time calls for it! The music really adds to the atmosphere in ways that you would never expect.

Verdict

Tangle Tower is a beautifully well-made game that does great justice for the murder-mystery genre. For that reason, I'm giving it a 10/10.
